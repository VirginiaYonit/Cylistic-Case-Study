## Business task
### Scenario

Cyclistic, a leading bike-share company in Chicago, has identified a significant opportunity to increase profitability by converting casual riders into annual members. **Financial analyses have demonstrated that annual members are more profitable than casual riders**. However, to develop effective marketing strategies aimed at this conversion, it is crucial to understand the differing usage patterns between these two user types. 

As a junior data analyst:"my primary task is to analyze historical trip data to identify how annual members and casual riders use Cyclist bikes differently", "this analysis will serve as the foundation for targeted marketing strategies designed to convert casual riders into annual members, thereby enhancing the company's profitability".

Project examined bicycle usage, pinpointing trends to transition casual riders to members, enhancing profitability. Findings are shared on Kaggle and GitHub.

The study scrutinized bicycle usage data, uncovering key variances in ride durations and peak usage between casual riders and members. Observations spurred targeted initiatives like promotional incentives and station upgrades, designed to shift casual riders to members, thus boosting profitability and achieving Cyclistic's business aims.

The analysis, conducted in R, initiated by defining the objective to elevate casual riders to members for increased profitability. Data was sourced from diverse origins, refined for uniformity, and evaluated to detect usage trends. Conclusions from visualizations prompted strategic suggestions like tailored promotions and infrastructure enhancements, which were communicated to stakeholders through various platforms.
